# iTruVoice 1.0.3

Fixes the long pause in the middle of comma-joined labels ("Accessibility,
back button"). The engine wraps every utterance it synthesizes in silence —
about 120 ms at the head and 379 ms at the tail, measured across ten words
and unchanged by rate, because it is not speech. VoiceOver sends such a label
as two pieces, so the two lots of padding met at the seam and left roughly
half a second of dead air.

Each piece is now trimmed to its speech, keeping 40 ms at either end so a
comma still reads as a comma; the junction lands at 80 ms, inside the
engine's own 90–180 ms between words. Only samples that are exactly zero are
removed, and bookmark positions shift with the audio so marks stay accurate.

Verified by 25 host checks (three new ones cover the trim, including that a
silent piece stays silent) and by rendered before/after audio.
