# iTruVoice 1.0.2

Fixes the speaking rate jumping between 50 and 60% on the VoiceOver slider.
The engine's rate knob goes to 400, but past 200 every setting produces the
same audio, with a cliff between 195 and 200 where one step gets 22% shorter
(measured, identical on every voice). The top of the slider mapped straight
into that dead zone. VoiceOver's 100 now maps to 195 — the fastest setting
that still answers — so every point on the slider changes the rate by the
same small step and the jump is gone.
